Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Deadzone2D uses, where it is stored and what rights you have.

1. Who is responsibleLume Studio INC

Deadzone2D is published by Lume Studio INC. The game works mostly offline and does not require an account on a server we own.

2. Data stored on your deviceLocal files

When you create a local account, the app saves on your PC: Gmail address, Nick, DisplayName, description, region, UserID, chosen icon and banner, creation date, Dead Coins, friends, read notifications and items bought in the Dead Royal store. Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are saved as a PBKDF2 hash with a salt.

Location: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Deadzone2D (accounts.json, announcements.json, notifications.json, admin.json, DeadRoyalShop.json and the media folder). These files survive game updates.

3. Google sign-inOptional

When you choose SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE, the app opens the official Google page. Google provides the game with your email address, name, account ID and profile picture. Deadzone2D never receives or stores your Google password and does not save the access token. Your Google email is used to identify your profile; an existing local account with the same email is reused.

The authorization response is received on a local port (127.0.0.1:53682) only during sign-in. A Client ID and Client Secret from Google Cloud must be configured in Config/Google.json.

4. Email verificationOptional

If SMTP is configured in Config/Mail.json, a 6-digit code is sent to the Gmail address you entered. The code expires after 5 minutes and is discarded after validation. Without configuration, the code is shown on screen for local testing.

5. How data is usedPurposes

Data is used only to authenticate the player, display the profile (status, UserID, account age and description), keep the local friend list, customize icons and banners, record Dead Royal purchases and show notifications and administrator announcements.

6. SharingNo central server

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. There is no central account server. The only external communication happens if you choose Google sign-in or if email sending is configured by you.

7. Game updatesGitHub Pages

On startup, the game checks a public update manifest on this site only to detect the available version. A new installer is downloaded only when you request the update.

8. PermissionsMinimal

The game does not request camera, microphone, location, device contacts or administrator access for data collection. The browser opens only when you choose Google sign-in.

9. Your rights (LGPD/GDPR)You control your data

You can view and correct your data in the in-game profile (DisplayName and description), delete a local account by removing the %LOCALAPPDATA%\Deadzone2D folder and revoke Deadzone2D access in your Google account settings. For help, open an issue in the project repository.

10. Changes2026-11-27

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with the update date.
